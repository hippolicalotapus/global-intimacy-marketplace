# ONE HEART — apparel-first launch and financing readiness
**Prepared:** 2026-10-31 · **Status:** Management working plan, pre-launch; not a loan application.
**Confidential/internal planning.** All prices except the expressly cited public benchmark are hypothetical; no products have been manufactured, quality approved, priced, or authorized for sales.

## Executive strategy
Launch ONE HEART as a testable Alberta-origin artwork and apparel line, initially using a low-inventory or print-on-demand approach. Focus on one hoodie and one art tee from the existing 16 lookbook design concepts. Continue broader marketplace development in parallel, using separate tracking for apparel revenue and expenses. Do not omit actual shared ownership, related operations, or proposed business activities when speaking with any lender. If a financing program requires a legally independent clothing operation, seek genuine advice on legal structuring and eligible use-of-funds before submitting an application.

## Milestone gates
1. **Rights and branding gate:** Confirm designers' rights to manufacture and license the ONE HEART artwork. Screen the name for trademark conflicts before ordering packaging, inventory or registering a mark. Official CIPO search: https://ised-isde.canada.ca/cipo/trademark-search/srch?lang=eng
2. **Manufacturing gate:** Request comparable quotations for (a) 1 sample and (b) 10 / 25 unit runs, for both hoodie and tee, including detailed print-area requirements, sizing, fabric composition/weight, laundering, colourfastness, return/warranty expectations, all-over print options and customer-facing shipping.
3. **Compliance gate:** Obtain proof of required Canadian apparel fibre/dealer labelling, accurate origin claims and accurate descriptions. Official: https://competition-bureau.canada.ca/en/labelling/textile-labelling/textile-labelling-requirements
4. **Economics gate:** Calculate real contribution per order, including production, setup, duty/tax differences, packaging, postage, processing, acquisition, refunds and returns; avoid confusing revenue with profit. Target a working contribution margin of >=25% after variable operating costs, but do not assert this is a grant/program eligibility condition. Test full-price demand before booking stock.
5. **Funding gate:** After registration/structure and costs are clear, submit a bank inquiry and seek coaching from Community Futures Yellowhead East. Applications, owner guarantees, collateral and credit checks require owner's informed approval.

## Registration and legal preparedness
Alberta business name registration is not the same as trademark registration or incorporation and does not automatically protect a name. An owner operating under a sole-proprietor trade name remains personally responsible for business debts. Official: https://www.alberta.ca/register-business-name . Explore legal form, trademark availability and any applicable municipal licensing before sales; do not independently bind owner to one legal structure.
