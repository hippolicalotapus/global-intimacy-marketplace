# Product-specific drawing accuracy standard — GIM
Status: Internal reference policy. Does not approve sale, photos, likenesses, or supplier affiliation.

## Required reference for every candidate
1. Record exact supplier, manufacturer, model, SKU, variant, colour, dimensions, materials and source URL. Distinguish verified manufacturer specifications from reseller descriptions.
2. Obtain written permission before reproducing supplier photographs, trademarks or exact protected visual assets. Use authorized references to guide an independently drawn depiction, not a disguised photograph.
3. Create a per-product drawing brief: front, back, side, key details, packaging and scale only where verified. Label unverified details as approximate and avoid inventing product features.
4. Compare finished drawing against the specific SKU's authorized reference; independently check shape, colours, proportions, textures, controls, seams, straps, hardware and included components.
5. Maintain a reference log with source date, authorization status, drawing version, reviewer, discrepancies, and approval. Keep imageApproved and resellerApproved false until separately verified.
6. For full-size dolls, confirm the depicted model is unambiguously adult, exact model/configuration, dimensions, skin tone, face and hair options, body proportions, pose and accessories. Do not imply a generic range illustration depicts a particular purchasable model.
7. For lingerie-on-doll composites, verify the lingerie SKU separately and describe the outfit as styling visualization unless fit/compatibility has been tested. Do not imply the lingerie is bundled with the doll.
8. For safety-relevant products, avoid guessing material safety, dimensions, instructions or functionality. Use verified specifications and clear uncertainty disclosures.

## Acceptance
- 1:1 verified SKU-to-drawing mapping, recorded reference provenance and discrepancy review.
- All front/back images legible and unclipped at 360px and 768px browser viewport sizes.
- No invented pricing, stock, reseller approval, bundling, or delivery promise.
- No live publishing of exact-likeness claims until review is signed off.
