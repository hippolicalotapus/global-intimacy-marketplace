# Vendor intake screening — internal draft
Date: 2026-11-08
Status: Internal planning only. Do not publish or collect applications yet.

## Initial screening fields (no sensitive documents at this stage)
Business display name; contact business email; website; operating country; business type (maker, boutique, wholesaler, distributor); product categories; 3–5 representative product URLs; fulfillment model (own stock, made to order, dropship); dispatch country and typical time; shipping destinations; return and defect handling; product media ownership or licensed usage; whether products require age checks or territory restrictions; wholesale or commission preference; integration type (manual, CSV, API); estimated active SKUs; minimum order terms.

## Evaluation rubric (100 points)
- Distinctiveness and hard-to-find value: 25
- Evidence of product authenticity and legal resale rights: 20
- Fulfillment reliability, transparent lead times and returns: 20
- Geographic reach and compliant shipping: 15
- Unit economics and viable margin after fees and returns: 15
- Product media and accurate variant data: 5

Thresholds: >=80 high-priority due diligence; 65–79 follow up for missing evidence; below 65 defer. No score constitutes legal, processor, identity or product approval.

## Hard stops
- Illegal or prohibited products or territories.
- Unverifiable rights to merchandise or imagery.
- Misleading inventory, materials, product origin or age suitability.
- No workable fulfillment and returns process.
- Unsupported processor category, payout jurisdiction or third-party seller arrangement.
- Vendor refuses required commercial identity verification during later onboarding.

## Commercial status
No vendor intake form live, no applications accepted, no fees charged, no sales permitted. Paid plans remain illustrative only. Do not request personal identity documents by ordinary email.
