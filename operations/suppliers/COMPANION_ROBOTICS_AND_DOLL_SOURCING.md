## Hard safety/legal/business launch gates
- Adult customers only; no minor-resembling or childlike dolls, products, AI personas or marketing. Canada's FINTRAC and criminal-law context show significant legal risk for childlike sex dolls, so prohibit this category categorically. Review every manufacturer model and optional customization before publish.
- No nonconsensual likeness/celebrity replicas or misleading marketing about human consent or identity.
- Verify CBSA customs and other local requirements by destination; no blanket promise of 'worldwide shipping'.
- Payment processor must **explicitly accept** the full adult-products and dolls business model in writing; Stripe's published restrictions include sexually oriented items. No payments until approval.
- Product images must be non-explicit and commercially licensed per manufacturer; default to abstract placeholders.
- A basic 18+ self-check is not age verification. Use suitable legal verification controls and appropriate region gating before transacting.
- For personalized AI companions: explicit, clear user consent for cameras, microphones, cloud storage and sensitive information; no unapproved remote recording/data processing.
- **Research previews only**: no add-to-cart, checkout, deposits, product reservation or promise of delivery. Existing demo wishlist sharing remains read-only; do not expose addresses.
