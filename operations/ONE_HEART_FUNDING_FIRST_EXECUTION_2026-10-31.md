### Cost/financing guardrails
Federal CSBFP can fund eligible working capital through term loans or a credit line, but the **2% registration fee, possible bank fees, security/personal guarantee, and interest** make small debt more expensive. Federal max $1.15m is NOT a suggested borrowing target. A $6,950 planning budget is not an approved requested amount; obtain costed SKU quotes, owner contribution, realistic sales and payment coverage first. As of Oct 31 neither a firm quote nor a registered legal structure, owner contribution or personal-finance documentation has been verified. A pre-revenue clothing startup's repayment projection must not assume sales that have not happened.
