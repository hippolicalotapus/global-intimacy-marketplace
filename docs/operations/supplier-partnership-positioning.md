# Supplier partnership positioning — approved 2026-11-12

Global Intimacy Marketplace is **building** an international marketplace for intimacy and lifestyle products, with a long-term ambition to offer one of the broadest selections across manufacturers, wholesalers, and independent vendors. The platform is in phased development; do not claim it is currently the largest, has confirmed global coverage, or has established sales volume.
