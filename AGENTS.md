# Global Intimacy Marketplace operating rules

The established production host is Netlify, publishing only `site/`. Preserve its existing GitHub deployment connection. Read the project handoff, approved feature list and current source before making changes. Distinguish published, tested locally, prepared privately and blocked work.

## Website releases

- Preserve the owner's approved original artwork, exact wording and prior feature approvals. Do not generate substitutes for approved logos.
- Maintain a release manifest comparing the actual production commit with local files. Review every meaningful difference, including directly accessible legacy assets; ignore retrieval-added trailing newlines.
- Complete the approved batch and appropriate tests before one production branch update. Consolidate related fixes to conserve Netlify credits. Do not claim that work or deployment is free or promise a credit balance without verified billing evidence.
- Verify the resulting public site and CI after publishing. Check key navigation, all product drawing coverage, complete hoodie fronts/backs, adult entry and country handling, and replay of the Ruby Heart reveal.
- Keep private supplier correspondence and operational records out of the public site and release tree. Research previews do not establish stock, pricing, resale permission, media rights or delivery eligibility.

## Supplier communication

- Before every proposed send, consult the latest private contact ledger and re-read fresh Gmail results across Inbox, Sent, Drafts and all related threads/aliases for that supplier.
- Compare the proposed questions with prior requests and replies. Omit answered questions; do not repeat an unanswered request while already awaiting a reply without a specific reason.
- Keep related requests in the existing thread. Prefer the actual requested artwork/files over inaccessible links. Confirm that a recipient can access files before claiming delivery.
- A receipt or ticket acknowledgement is not a substantive reply or approval. Do not infer permission from silence.
- Log a send only after the service confirms it, including message/thread ID, date, exact recipient, questions and attachment versions. Never retry an uncertain send until Sent has been checked.
- Read-only review does not authorize a new message, purchase, paid sample, contract, account terms or identity-document disclosure. Use the owner's actual authorization for each action.

Update the private handoff and ledger after work so the next session does not repeat completed actions.
