About the beta

Can I buy products today?

Not yet. Products are unpriced research previews. Purchasing and dropshipping will be enabled only after we verify authorized suppliers, stock, image licenses and a suitable payment provider.

Are wishlists free?

Our plan is to provide eligible adult creators with complimentary wishlist accounts. Right now, you can try a browser-only demonstration without sharing personal delivery details.

Is Global Intimacy Marketplace associated with a creator platform?

No. Global Intimacy Marketplace is independent and is not an official partner of OnlyFans, Fansly, Stripchat, Chaturbate or any manufacturer.

Where is the business based?

We're developing this business from Alberta, Canada, with international sourcing and global market ambitions.
